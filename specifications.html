Materials & construction

3g

Wing weight

The fully deployed 2–3 wing configuration weighs approximately three grams.

The wing configuration is a compressible, collapsible rubber polymer — available in Silicone, TPU, PVC, and Latex. It is a backup retention mechanism only: the catheter’s primary stability comes from firm adherence to the patient’s thigh, using a Lotus Stabilizer or cross adhesive tape.

Independent Bench Testing

Balloon dislodgement force: full results

63 siliconized latex Foley catheters, sizes 14–26 Fr, tested per ASTM F623-99 §4.3 (Inflated Balloon Response to Traction) in an FDA-registered, ISO 9000 laboratory. A 28 Fr funnel-like apparatus simulated the bladder outlet and urethra; load was applied at ~155 mm/s until the balloon pulled through. For comparison, the Lotus Catheter’s 2–3 wing configuration requires only 2–3 lb (8.9–13.3 N) to dislodge.

Directions for Use

1. Before insertion

Activate the bellows before insertion: grasp both ends and pull until fully open to confirm deployment, then push together until fully closed
Generously lubricate the tip and shaft using aseptic technique
Do not use petroleum-based ointments or lubricants
Sterile if package is unopened or undamaged — single use only

2. Insertion & deployment

Insert the catheter tip; once fluid flow confirms bladder placement, advance 2–3 inches further to ensure proper wing deployment
Activate the bellows again, gently pulling until fully open
Pull gently outward until resistance is felt — the wings are now at the bladder neck. Do not stabilize to the thigh yet
Advance 2.5–5.0 cm toward the patient so the wings clear the trigone, then secure the bellows firmly to the thigh

3. During use & removal

If the patient feels discomfort, the wings are likely resting on the trigone — close the wings, advance 2.5–5.0 cm further, then reopen and re-stabilize
To remove: release from the stabilizer, gently push the bellows together until fully compressed, then slowly withdraw
If resistance is encountered during removal, cut the catheter shaft and activation rod to collapse the wings and free the device
Indwelling up to 30 days. Discard in accordance with medical facility safety procedures

Storage, handling & safety

Download full IFU

Storage conditions

Store in a dark, cool, dry place. Avoid freezing and excessive heat.

Single-use, sterile device

Do not re-sterilize or reprocess. Reuse can result in hazard to the patient. Do not use after the expiration date.

Pediatric use

Approved for patients 5 years of age or older. Federal (USA) law restricts this device to sale by or on the order of a physician.
